Paint Mix privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

In short: Paint Mix has no accounts and collects no personal data. Your progress and settings stay on your iPhone. We don't track you, and this version shows no third-party ads.

Who we are

Paint Mix is published by Buddy Apps ("we", "us"). For any privacy question, contact us through the Paint Mix support page.

What stays on your iPhone

To work, the game saves a few things in its own storage on your device:

  • your progress: the levels you finished, your stars, and the paintings in your Gallery
  • your profile: the name and avatar you pick, your stats (wins, streaks, pours) and achievements
  • your settings: sound, music and vibration
  • whether you bought Remove Ads, and counters that space out ads (for example, the number of wins since the last ad)

This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can erase it at any time by deleting the app. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, Apple may include it in that backup under Apple's privacy policy.

What we collect

Nothing. Paint Mix has no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising identifiers and no servers of its own. The game works offline.

Purchases

The optional Remove Ads purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment details. The app only asks Apple whether Remove Ads has been bought on your Apple ID, for example when you tap Restore purchases.

Ads

This version of Paint Mix shows no ads from ad networks and does not track you across apps or websites. If we add ads from an ad network in a future version, we will update this policy first, say which partners we use and what they collect, and ask for your consent where the law requires it (including Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt).

Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. They do not identify you, and we use them only to fix bugs.

Children

Paint Mix is a game for a general audience and is not made specifically for children. Because it collects no personal data, it collects none from children either.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU and UK GDPR or California law), you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data and to object to its use. Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to send or delete: the data described above exists only on your iPhone and is removed when you delete the app. You can still contact us with any question, and if you are in the EU you can complain to your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

Changes to this policy

If we change how Paint Mix handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches the app.
