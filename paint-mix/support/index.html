Questions and answers

How do I play?

Tap a tube to pick up its top drop, then tap a cup to drop it in (or drag the drop onto a cup). When a cup is full, the paint mixes: red + yellow = orange, yellow + blue = green, red + blue = purple. If the painting needs that color, it paints a region. Fill every region to win. Tap How to play on the home screen, or ? during a level, for an animated demo.

A cup turned grey with an ✕. What happened?

The mix wasn't a color the painting needs, so it turned to mud and blocked the cup. Use Undo to take the pour back, or Rinse to empty the cup.

There's no sound.

Paint Mix follows your iPhone's Silent mode, so sound and music are muted while the Ring/Silent switch (or Action button) is set to Silent. Also check the Sound and Music switches in the game's Settings ⚙ and your iPhone's volume.

I bought Remove Ads on another device or reinstalled the game.

Open Settings ⚙ in the game and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

My progress is gone.

Progress is saved only on your iPhone. Deleting the app erases it, unless you restore your iPhone from a backup made before that.

I'm color-blind. Can I play?

Yes. Every color has its own symbol (▲ red, ● yellow, ■ blue, and mixes combine their symbols), so you can play by shape alone.

What data does the game collect?

None. See the privacy policy.
